About Us

Built by our community, for our community.

The Spectrum Club of NWI started with a simple belief: every individual deserves the opportunity to be themselves, build meaningful relationships, and know that they are never alone.

We create a welcoming environment across Northwest Indiana where autistic adults can come together, take part in activities, build friendships, and experience a true sense of belonging, and where families can connect with others who understand.

Our MissionCreating connections, building friendships, and making the world a more understanding and inclusive place for everyone.

  • Belonging

    A judgment-free space where you can come as you are.

  • Friendship

    Real connections with people who share your interests.

  • Family Support

    A network of parents and caregivers who understand.

  • Local Fun

    Get-togethers and events right here in NWI.

Our Team

The people behind the club.

For each of us, this mission is personal.

Zachary Jatkiewicz

Board Member

A passionate autism advocate on the spectrum himself, Zachary brings a personal perspective and a deep understanding of the importance of acceptance, inclusion, and support.

Read Zachary's story

Growing up on the autism spectrum shaped Zachary's passion for making a difference in the lives of others. Through his own experiences, he understands that autistic individuals deserve more than just acceptance; they deserve opportunities to build friendships, develop meaningful relationships, participate in their communities, and live fulfilling lives.

Zachary is currently pursuing an education in Social Work, where he hopes to gain the knowledge, skills, and experience to better support individuals and families. He hopes to use his education and personal experiences to advocate for individuals, connect them with resources, and help create opportunities for a better quality of life, for children and adults on the spectrum alike.

Through The Spectrum Club of NWI, Zachary hopes to help create a welcoming environment where autistic adults can come together, participate in activities, build friendships, and experience a true sense of belonging.

Zachary also organizes an annual 5K Autism Advocacy Run/Walk every April through his own foundation, the Jatkiewicz Foundation, in recognition of Autism Awareness Month. The event brings families, individuals, supporters, and community members together to promote awareness, encourage acceptance, and show support.

He is committed to speaking up for individuals on the autism spectrum whose experiences and needs are not always understood. He believes autistic individuals should have a voice in decisions that affect their lives and should never be defined by their diagnosis.

Kathleen Jatkiewicz

Board Member

A dedicated mother, advocate, and supporter of individuals on the autism spectrum, Kathleen's passion comes from raising her son, Zachary, who is on the spectrum.

Read Kathleen's story

Through her journey as a mother, Kathleen has gained a firsthand understanding of the challenges, experiences, and opportunities that autistic individuals and their families may face.

Her experiences inspired her to return to school to pursue a degree in education. She believes that education, patience, understanding, and compassion can make a meaningful difference in the lives of individuals with autism.

Today, Kathleen is passionate about extending that same love, encouragement, and support to other individuals on the spectrum, particularly adults. She believes autistic adults deserve opportunities to build friendships, develop independence, discover their abilities, and become active members of their communities.

Through The Spectrum Club of NWI, Kathleen hopes to help create a welcoming and supportive environment where autistic adults feel accepted, valued, understood, and included. She believes every individual deserves the opportunity to be themselves, build meaningful relationships, and know that they are never alone.

Dawn Kazmierski

Founder & Board Member

"Starting this club is deeply personal to me and brings a longtime dream to life. Friendships are important."

Read Dawn's story

Hi! I'm Dawn Kazmierski, and I'm thrilled to be the Founder and Board Member of The Spectrum Club of NWI. Starting this club is deeply personal to me and brings a longtime dream to life. Friendships are important. I'm passionate about creating fun, low-stress gatherings, from board game nights to casual meet-ups, where autistic adults can connect naturally and make lasting friends.

Over the past 14+ years, I've spent my time volunteering across all of my boys' schools, as well as several years working directly with students as a Recess Aide at a local elementary school.

Outside of the club, I enjoy spending time with my family, traveling, listening to music, all things Disney and Universal, and plenty of other fun, silly adventures!

In consideration of being permitted to participate in activities, events, and programs organized by The Spectrum Club of NWI ("the Club"), I, the undersigned, acknowledge and agree to the following:

2. Release. I release the Club, its organizers, volunteers, and partners from any and all claims arising from participation, except in cases of gross negligence.

3. Medical. I authorize Club organizers to seek emergency medical treatment for me if it is needed during a Club activity.

4. Photos & social media. I understand photos and videos may be taken at Club events. If I give permission below, photos and videos that include me may be shared on the Club's website and social media pages (such as Facebook). I can change my mind at any time by telling an organizer.
